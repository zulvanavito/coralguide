# DESIGN.md: Coral Island Companion

## 1. Identity & Personality
- **Product:** Coral Island Companion
- **Core Value:** "Help players quickly find game information and never forget where they left off."
- **Personality:** Warm, organized, ocean-tropical, dependable, and calm.
- **Role:** Personal game journal & active companion, not a cluttered wiki clone.

## 2. Color Palette
Derived directly from Coolors (`2f7d72-abe9f0-eff5f5-1d93a8-917e68`):
- **Background (`#EFF5F5`):** Soft pale mint / off-white, calm and gentle on the eyes during long gaming sessions.
- **Pine Sea Green (`#2F7D72`):** Primary brand tone, headers, major action buttons, and active chips. (WCAG AAA text variant: `#235E56` with 6.79:1 contrast).
- **Soft Aqua Seafoam (`#ABE9F0`):** Subdued borders, secondary card outlines, chip outlines.
- **Tropical Ocean Teal (`#1D93A8`):** Vibrant interactive accent, `+1 Day` button, active task checkboxes, and progress bar fill.
- **Warm Sand Driftwood (`#917E68` / `#6D5E4D`):** Earthy natural tones for session timestamps, metadata, and warm companion notes.
- **Card Background (`#FFFFFF`):** Clean, crisp surface for optimal readable content.

## 3. Typography
- **Heading:** Bold, clean sans-serif with strong hierarchy.
- **Body:** Legible, balanced line-height for mobile screens.
- **Rule R-02:** Strict zero em dash (`—`) policy. Use commas, periods, colons, or parentheses.

## 4. Dials (Antislop Liveliness)
- **ENERGY:** 3/5 (Balanced: playful tropical vibes without distracting from quick game data lookup).
- **RHYTHM:** 3/5 (Dynamic: cards, chips, progress indicators, clear visual breathing room).
- **MOTION:** 2/5 (Fast & functional: snappy modal presentations, instant tap feedback).

## 5. Craftsmanship & Principles
- **Intentionality:** Every button, chip, and badge serves a concrete in-game player need.
- **Offline-First:** 100% functional without internet connectivity.
- **Quick Capture:** Task and note creation under 3 seconds.
